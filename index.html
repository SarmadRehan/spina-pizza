The Menu

The menu rotates, so ask about tonight's specials.

Pies

Wood-fired, 12 inches, finished with a generous snowfall of 30-month Parmigiano Reggiano.

  • Margherita

    Proofed dough and fresh mozz. The classic.

  • Funghi

    The mushroom pie.

  • Hot Boy

    Pepperoni and hot honey.

  • Pepperoni & Ricotta

    Pepperoni, piped ricotta, basil and Parmigiano.

  • Diavola

    Slathered in Calabrian pesto, piled high with shallots and sausage. Calabrian ranch on the side if you need it.

  • The Capo

    Prosciutto di Parma, gorgonzola, hand-sliced pear, shallots, roasted garlic crème fraîche, vincotto and 30-month Parmigiano Reggiano. Their most labor-intensive pie.

Pasta & Big Plates

  • Rigatoni

    Old World pasta in a heavy marinara.

  • Lasagna for 2

    Two days in the making: Sunday gravy, beef and pork Bolognese, fresh mozz, 30-month Reggiano and a dab of pesto, with house focaccia for scarpetta.

  • Branzino Divorciado

    Butterflied and wood-fired crispy. Half asparagus-basil pesto, half Calabrian fig jam and berbere pesto.

  • Braised Beef RagùRecent special

    Braised with red wine, cherry tomato and Sicilian oregano, over creamy polenta with Pecorino Romano.

Small Plates

  • Stuffed Long Hot Peppers

    Sausage and provolone stuffed, in a bed of Sunday gravy, with focaccia.

  • Caesar Salad

  • Fig CarpaccioSeasonal

    Black Mission figs with stracciatella, good olive oil, Meyer lemon and Maldon salt.

Sweet

  • Tiramisu

    House-made.

  • Soft Serve

    A rotating cast of flavors, on the late-night menu.

Our story

A Family of Pizza Makers

Chef and owner Anthony Spina Jr. comes from a family of pizza makers: his father started O4W Pizza.

After about five years of delays and a run of pop-ups, Spina finally opened at the Atlanta Dairies on August 27, 2026.
